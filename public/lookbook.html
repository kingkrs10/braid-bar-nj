Our Story

Upscale Protective Styling, Rooted in Love

Hey y'all, hey! I'm Sharon French, a self-taught braider with over 20 years of experience. Owner of Apple Butter Boutique and the Braid Bar, a style haven located in the valley district of West Orange, New Jersey.

At The Braid Bar, every appointment is an experience — from the moment you walk in to the moment you leave feeling like your most confident self. We specialize in knotless braids, feed-in cornrows, crochet styles, locs, and VIP luxury braiding experiences using both premium synthetic and 100% human hair.

Our mission is simple: protect your crown while making you feel like royalty.
